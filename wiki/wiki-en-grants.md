## 📋 Project Justification Highlights

- **Scientific Value:** Real-time orbital physics, NEO tracking, and galactic topology simulation.
- **Educational Value:** Interactive 3D tool accessible to students, teachers, and researchers without specialized hardware.
- **Cultural Value:** Democratizing access to cosmological simulation for citizens and institutions.
- **Technological Value:** Advanced WebGL rendering engine with custom topology physics (DVTRGAS).
- **International Collaboration:** Multi-language support and open proposal system.
